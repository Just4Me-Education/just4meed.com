Privacy notice

Just4Me Education does not ask for or collect personal information on this website. There are no accounts, sign-up forms, analytics or ads, and we do not set cookies. The Level Setting reading and math checks run entirely in your browser: what you type and your student's answers stay on that page, are never sent to us or anyone else, and are deleted when you close the page or press Delete. If you download the results, the file is saved only on your device. Our sample videos are hosted on YouTube and load only when you press play. At that point YouTube (Google) receives your IP address and browser details and may store cookies or similar data on your device, as described in Google's Privacy Policy. Like any website, our host, GitHub Pages, receives your IP address in order to deliver the page and may keep it briefly in its server logs. We will update this notice before we add accounts or collect any information about you or your student. Questions? Email memberservice@just4meed.com.
