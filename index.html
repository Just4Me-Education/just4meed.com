What we're building

Four promises guide everything we make

Embrace the difference of every student

No one-size-fits-all workbook. Tell us what each student enjoys, how they read, and what they need, and every lesson is built for that student. Siblings each get their own plan.

Empower parents, don’t try to replace them

You can read, change, or swap any lesson before your student sees it. You decide what is taught and when.

Accurate and aligned to standards

Lessons are matched to your state's learning standards, and facts are checked against trusted sources before they reach you.

Private by design

Built to follow COPPA, the U.S. law that protects children's privacy online. Students use a nickname, and their information is never sold or used for ads.

For students who learn differently

Lessons that adjust to your student's needs

Many families teach a student who doesn't fit a standard curriculum. Just4Me is designed so every lesson considers your student's strengths and challenges and brings out their best.

Reading support

Just4Me can create easy-to-read type, shorter passages, and text read aloud, for students with dyslexia or who read below grade level. More advanced exercises can be created to keep students reading above grade level interested.

Attention and focus

By basing lessons on your student’s interests, they are more likely to stay engaged with the exercise. Just4Me also can use short steps, frequent breaks, and hands-on activities, for students with ADHD or short attention spans.

Pace and level

Slow down, speed up or repeat any subject, so students at different levels don’t become bored or overwhelmed.

Interests as a way in

Lessons built around what your student loves, from fossils to trains, to make learning feel engaging.

Different ways to show learning

Students can answer by talking, drawing, or building, not only by writing.

Notes for you

Each day includes tips for the parent on how to adjust the lesson if your student gets stuck.

Just4Me is a teaching tool. It works alongside, and does not replace, any therapy, evaluation, or specialist support your student already has.

How it will work

From a few answers to a week of lessons

  1. Set the stage for success

    Set lessons to grade, interests, reading level, and anything that helps your student learn. Use a nickname: we never ask for a name, photo, address, or anything else that identifies your student.

  2. Get a week of lessons

    Daily plans with clear goals, timed steps, activities, and short videos.

  3. Review and adjust

    Look over each day, make changes, and decide what your student sees.

  4. Teach and track progress

    Your student works through the lessons, and you see how each one went. Just4Me will produce daily, weekly and full end of marking period reports that can be submitted to support your state requirements.

Our privacy promise

Your family's information stays your family's

We are building privacy from the start, not adding it later.

  • Nicknames onlyWe never need a student's full name, photo, or school.
  • No ads and no selling dataYour information is used to make your lessons and nothing else.
  • Parents hold the keysYou can see, download, or delete your data at any time.
  • No tricksNo streak pressure, countdown timers, or widgets meant to keep students hooked.
