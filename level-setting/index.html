Level Setting: find your student's starting point

These short checks show where your student is in reading or math, so lessons can start at the right level. Your student answers on their own, and the check moves up or down a level as they go. When they finish, you get a results page with strengths and ideas for what to practice. It is not a grade, and there is no pass or fail.

No account needed. Use a nickname only. Nothing you enter is saved or sent anywhere.

How it works

Four steps

  1. Tell us about your student

    Nickname, grade, attention span and any learning needs. These change how the check is shown.

  2. Hand over the device

    Your student answers on their own, with breaks whenever they need one.

  3. See the results

    Press and hold the grown-ups button to open the results when your student is done.

  4. Download, then delete

    Download the results page or save it as a PDF. Then delete everything with one button, or just close the page.

Your privacy

Results are shown one time and kept nowhere

Everything happens on this page in your browser. We don't save or receive your answers or results. Closing the page, or pressing Delete when you finish, removes everything.
