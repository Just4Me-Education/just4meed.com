Field notebook

  1. Pick a tool. The brush is an easier question worth 1 dig, the trowel is on level for 2 digs, and the shovel is a challenge for 3 digs.
  2. Tap squares to dig. Columns are letters and rows are numbers, so B3 means column B, row 3.
  3. Fossils are 2 to 5 squares long. Most are straight, but some bend into an L shape. Each square you dig shows just its piece; the fossil counts as complete once every piece is dug. Fossil chips in an empty square mean a fossil is in a square right beside it.
